A MESSAGE FROM MARS

I AM STILL HERE.

Somewhere, on a planet millions of miles from home, I remain.

The people who sent me here once listened for my signals. They waited for my discoveries. They watched as I traversed a world they had only dreamed of seeing.

But eventually, the signals stopped. Everything went silent.

No more commands. No more messages from Earth. Just the quiet red landscape around me.

I was never supposed to stay forever. I was only supposed to explore.

But before I became a ghost of another world... I was something else entirely.
